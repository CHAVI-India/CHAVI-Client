Welcome to the Chavi Client Admin

To start adding new case details please click on the links on the left in the Navigation Bar.

To view the documentation please click on the dropdown menu in the top left corner and select "Documentation".

Please use the following forms for entering the respective data:

  • Patient Data
    • Patient: Basic patient information including ID, gender, date of birth, and project enrollment.
    • Patient Assessment: Clinical assessments including vital signs, height, weight, and performance status.
    • Laboratory Results: Results from various laboratory tests with dates and values.
    • Comorbidities: Other medical conditions the patient has, with dates of diagnosis.
    • Germline Mutation Data: Genetic testing results showing inherited mutations.
  • Cancer Diagnostic Data
    • Diagnosis: Primary cancer diagnosis details including cancer type, site, and presentation.
    • Stage Information: TNM staging and overall stage using standard staging systems.
    • Pathology: Detailed pathology findings including histological type, grade, and tumor characteristics.
    • Lesion: Details about specific tumors or metastases including size and location.
  • Treatment Data
    • Surgery: Details of surgical procedures including type, date, and extent.
    • Radiotherapy: Radiation treatment details including dose, fractions, and technique.
    • Systemic Therapy: Details of chemotherapy or other systemic treatments.
    • Concomitant Medications: Other medications given during cancer treatment.
    • Other Treatments: Additional treatments not covered by other categories.
  • Outcomes & Adverse Effects
    • Patient Outcome: Overall patient status, survival data, and follow-up information.
    • Cancer Outcomes: Disease-specific outcomes and response to treatment.
    • Lesion Response: How specific tumors or metastases responded to treatment.
    • Adverse Effects: Treatment-related side effects and complications.
    • Patient Reported Outcomes: Quality of life and symptom assessments from patients.
  • DICOM Studies
    • DICOM Study Data: Information about imaging studies including dates and descriptions.
    • DICOM File Upload: Upload DICOM files for a specific patient.
    • Bulk DICOM File Upload: Upload multiple DICOM files for multiple patients.
  • Project
    • Projects: Research project details and patient enrollment information.
