Patient Outcomes
==============

The Patient Outcomes module tracks the overall status and outcomes of patients throughout their cancer journey.

Data Collection Fields
--------------------

Status Information
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
* **Patient Link**: The patient whose outcome is being recorded
* **Patient Status**: Last known status from standardized list
* **Last Follow-up Date**: Date of most recent contact/information

Mortality Information
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
* **Date of Death**: If applicable
* **Cancer-Related**: Whether death was related to cancer progression
* **Last Known Status**: Final documented condition

Special Considerations
--------------------

1. Status Updates:

   #. Record changes in status promptly  
   #. Document all follow-up attempts  
   #. Note any loss to follow-up  

2. Documentation:

   #. Maintain accurate timeline  
   #. Record cause of death when known
